**Brand:** AI Engineering Studio  
**Positioning:** We build AI agents, business automations, web apps, and mobile apps for ambitious businesses.  
**Target:** Pune SMBs, startups, and tech-forward businesses needing AI + custom software.  
**Differentiator:** Direct senior engineer access, fixed-scope pricing, bi-weekly sprints.
